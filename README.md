A program that writes one funny excuse, in a dog's own voice, to `/output/entry.txt`,
for the make.fun competition
[The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-the-dog-ate-my-homework-funniest-excuse-8659c98c).

Run it with Python 3:

```
python3 main.py
```

It writes the excuse below to `/output/entry.txt` (or to the path given as
the first argument, or `$OUTPUT_PATH`). No network, no clock, no
randomness. The excuse is 111 characters, inside the 1 to 200 gate.

## The excuse

```
The homework said show your working, so I did. I worked on it with my teeth, and the answers are inside me now.
```

## Why this excuse

The judge's brief, in order: it must be an excuse a dog gives after being
caught, in the dog's own voice; it must earn the bigger laugh with a twist
or a piece of dog logic; it must sound like a real dog; it must work on its
own; it must feel fresh.

- The dog takes "show your working" literally and works on the homework
  with its teeth, then insists the answers are safe inside it now: a dog
  who believes it has done nothing wrong, and even helped.
- No stock line about the cat, the taste of paper, or being a good boy.
- One line, no setup, nothing that needs a second line to make sense.
